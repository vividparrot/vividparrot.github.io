Assumptions and limitations
Estimate for the New Tax Regime only (default from FY 2025-26). Assumes a ₹75,000 standard deduction for salaried/pensioners and full Section 87A rebate for taxable income up to ₹12,00,000, with marginal relief just above the threshold. Surcharge on high incomes (10% above ₹50L, 15% above ₹1cr, 25% above ₹2cr, with marginal relief) is applied, and cess is levied on tax plus surcharge. This is an educational estimate, not tax advice — consult a professional or the official Income Tax portal for filing.
